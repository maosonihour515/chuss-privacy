CHUSS Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 (updated: cloud backup of game progress)

Summary (plain language)

  • Local, vs CPU, Practice, the tutorial, and the Daily Challenge work entirely on your device. We never see anything about how you play them.
  • Your settings stay on your phone. Your Shells, Shop items, daily streak, and puzzle stars are also saved on your phone — and if you're signed in, backed up to your account so they follow you to another phone.
  • Online play and cloud backup need an account (a username and password — never a real email address). We store your username, chosen avatar, skill rating, and, while you're signed in, your game progress.
  • While you're in a live online match, your moves are sent to the one other player you're matched with, in real time, so the match stays in sync. We don't keep this after the match ends.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no data sold to anyone.
  • You can change your display name and avatar any time in the Profile screen, and delete your account entirely, right there in the app (see "Your Rights & Choices" below).

1. Scope

This policy covers the CHUSS mobile app (Android and iOS), desktop app, and web version, made by Mao Sonihour ("we," "us"). It applies to every part of the app: Local (hotseat, same device), vs CPU (against computer opponents), Practice and the Daily Challenge (solo), the tutorial, and Online (real-time matches against another player).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information (Online mode only)

Creating an Online account requires:

  • A username you choose (used to sign in — we never ask for or collect a real email address; sign-in works entirely on username + password).
  • A password, stored in hashed form by our authentication provider — we never see or store your plaintext password.
  • An avatar you pick from a fixed set of presets (not an uploaded image).
  • Your skill rating, which changes automatically based on match outcomes.

2.1a Game progress (only while signed in)

While you're signed in, your game progress is backed up to your account so it survives reinstalling and follows you to another phone: your Shells balance (as running totals of what each of your phones has earned and spent), the Shop items you've unlocked and equipped, your daily-reward streak, the last day you cleared the Daily Challenge, whether you've finished the tutorial, and your best stars on each puzzle level, plus a random ID for each phone so progress from several phones can be combined. This contains nothing about you beyond your play in CHUSS.

2.2 Live match data (Online mode only)

While an online match is in progress, your in-game actions (aim, shot power, position) are relayed in real time to the one opponent you're currently matched with, so both sides see the same match. This data is not written to any database and is not retained after the match ends.

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect or access: your real name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising identifiers, or payment information. CHUSS has no in-app purchases and shows no ads. We use no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

2.4 Offline modes

Local, vs CPU, Practice, the tutorial, and the Daily Challenge run entirely on your device. No account is needed, and nothing about your play in these modes is sent to us or anyone else.

2.5 Data kept on your device

The app saves a few things in your phone's own storage so they're there next time: your settings (sound, vibration, aim, graphics, language) and your game progress (Shells, Shop items, streak, Daily Challenge and puzzle progress). Settings are never sent to us. Game progress is sent to us only while you're signed in, as described in 2.1a — if you never sign in, it stays on your device. Android's own backup may include it in your Google account backup, under Google's terms, if you have device backup turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Let you sign in and keep your Online profile (username, avatar, rating) consistent across sessions.
  • Back up your game progress and combine it across your phones, while you're signed in.
  • Match you with an opponent and keep a live online match in sync.
  • Calculate rating changes after an online match.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Who We Share It With

  • Our backend provider (Supabase). Account and profile data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, acting as our data processor. Supabase's own privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.
  • The player you're matched with online. Your username, avatar, and rating are visible to whoever you're currently playing against in Online mode — that's how the game shows who you're facing.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data (username, avatar, rating) and backed-up game progress are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Live match data is never persisted — it exists only for the duration of the match.
  • If you delete your account (see below), your account data is permanently removed.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Update your profile. Change your display name and avatar any time from the Profile screen.
  • Delete your account. Open Profile → Delete Account, and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data (username, avatar, rating, and backed-up game progress), and resets the game progress saved on that phone — there is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone. You can also email maosonihour.mail@gmail.com if you'd rather we do it for you.
  • Access or export your data. Email us at maosonihour.mail@gmail.com and we'll provide a copy of the account data we hold about you.

These rights apply regardless of where you live; if you're in the EU/UK, EEA, or California, you may also have additional rights (such as data portability) under GDPR or CCPA — contact us and we'll accommodate applicable requests.

7. Children's Privacy

CHUSS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at maosonihour.mail@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

We rely on our backend provider's industry-standard security practices (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access-controlled database rules) to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we don't hold any more information than the game actually needs to function.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in-app.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data: maosonihour.mail@gmail.com
